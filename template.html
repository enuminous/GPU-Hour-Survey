How the average is calculated

Each qualifying provider contributes one published per-GPU hourly quote for the selected GPU family. We take an equally weighted arithmetic mean across those providers, then report the median and range.

The default excludes preemptible / spot, marketplace inventory medians, “from” prices, subscription-restricted offers, and commitments. Check Include indicative to inspect a broader reference set; its headline becomes indicative, not directly comparable.

Model families may contain SXM, PCIe, HGX, or unspecified variants. A per-GPU rate on a multi-GPU machine does not mean you can rent one card. Hardware matching, compute allocation and regional pricing remain limitations.

Important boundaries

  • No claims of real-time capacity, guaranteed booking, or transaction-level prices.
  • Provider pages are authoritative for their own quoted prices, not for the whole market.
  • Sample averages are not weighted by market share or actual transaction volume.
  • Taxes, storage, network egress, CPU/RAM allocation, minimum hours, contract terms, and regional availability can change the final bill.
  • Review snapshot dates before use. Edit data/prices.json and rerun the build to refresh.
